Refund Policy

1. General Policy

Refunds are only possible if the product has not been delivered. Once the license key or digital product has been sent to the provided email address or contact information, no refunds will be issued.

2. Delivery Issues

If you have not received your product, please contact us within 48 hours with valid proof of purchase. After verification, we will—at our sole discretion—either resend the product or issue a refund.

3. Chargebacks

Any attempt to initiate a chargeback will result in:

  • A permanent ban from all our services.
  • Immediate revocation of all purchased licenses.
  • A formal dispute of the chargeback claim, supported by proof of delivery.

We reserve the right to pursue legal action in the event of a fraudulent chargeback.

4. Non-Refundable Items

Due to the nature of digital goods, all successfully delivered products are strictly non-refundable.

We may update this Refund Policy at any time. It is your responsibility to check this page for any updates. Continued use of our services constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
